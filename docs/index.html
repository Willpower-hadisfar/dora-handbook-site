Compliance is not resilience

Under DORA, the regulatory clock starts at technical detection, not when General Counsel convenes.

Most financial institutions treat Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 as a legal documentation exercise. Supervisors audit the opposite: whether your telemetry classifies a major incident inside four hours, whether your Register of Information maps every fourth-party cloud dependency, and whether your live failover actually holds under stress. This handbook translates every chapter of DORA into concrete architecture, telemetry, and CI/CD controls.

From sprawl to proof

DORA replaces fragmented national banking circulars with one directly applicable European rulebook across twenty-one categories of financial entities and their critical ICT providers.

Inventory

Map every critical or important function, underlying microservice, database, and third-party cloud dependency into one authoritative graph.

Instrument

Wire incident classification, RTO/RPO assertions, and immutable audit trails directly into runtime telemetry and CI/CD gates.

Prove

Run scheduled failover drills, Threat-Led Penetration Tests, and exit rehearsals that produce regulator-ready evidence automatically.
